How the score works

50 points · Savings rate

Up to 50 points when you save 20% or more of monthly income. No points when spending equals or exceeds income.

50 points · Budget control

50 points when spending stays at or below 80% of budget; decreases linearly to zero at 100% or above.

This is an educational indicator based on expenses recorded in Pocket IQ 2.0 for the current calendar month. It is not professional financial advice.
